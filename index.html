About Me

I am a fresh graduate with a Bachelor’s degree in Physics from Universitas Airlangga, specializing in Computational Physics, with a strong interest in data analysis and how data can be transformed into meaningful insights for better decision-making. My background in physics has trained me to think analytically, systematically, and evidence-based, while my experience in computational physics has strengthened my ability to work with data and apply structured approaches to problem-solving.

As I develop my career as a Data Analyst, I work with Excel, SQL, and Power BI for data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, data processing, visualization, and insight generation. I also use Python, particularly Pandas and NumPy, to process and analyze data, along with Matplotlib to communicate findings through clear visualizations. Through various projects, I have developed experience in transforming raw data into structured information and identifying meaningful patterns, trends, and insights.

I believe data analysis is not only about numbers and visualizations, but also about understanding what the data can tell us and communicating those findings clearly. By combining my physics background, computational skills, and growing expertise in data analysis, I aim to become a Data Analyst who delivers accurate, clear, and actionable insights that support better decision-making.
